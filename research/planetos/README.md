## `agot-chapter-coverage.csv`

Working chapter coverage ledger for the book continuity of *A Game of Thrones*. It contains **73 narrative chapters**: the prologue (`chapter_no=0`) and 72 numbered chapters. The AWOIAF reference module also lists an appendix; it is not a narrative chapter and is intentionally excluded. A category count of 74 pages therefore must not be treated as 74 chapters.

Chapter order, titles, POV labels, and canonical numeric chapter-page links are indexed from [AWOIAF, Chapters Table of Contents](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Chapters_Table_of_contents) and cross-checked against [AWOIAF, Module:Ref/ASOIAF/data](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Module%3ARef%2FASOIAF%2Fdata) (accessed 2026-11-24). AWOIAF is a secondary locator, not the final authority for facts. Verify chapter content in the chosen book edition before marking a row reviewed.

Current snapshot `2026.11.24.41` contains 1,107 cards (535 people, 229 places, 41 organizations, 302 events) across 67 Planetos layers. The *A Game of Thrones* ledger has 2,593 chapter links and 621 unique refs (342 people, 109 places, 23 organizations, 147 events); every ref resolves and no relation field contains duplicate refs. The *A Clash of Kings* ledger has 1,059 links and 370 unique refs across all 70 chapters; all refs resolve. Its reconciled secondary candidate inventory separates 195 human identities, 4 animals, 8 dream-only figures, 72 physical places, 19 groups, 65 state-classified events, 3 dream events, and 3 context-only event references; Orell ch.51 and the Thirteen of Qarth remain ambiguous. The denominator report and full candidate reconciliation are in [`ACOK candidate denominator`](audits/acok/2026-11-24/candidate-denominator.md). For ASOS, four range audits and their global reconciliation yield 772 candidate entities (429 people, 114 places, 162 events, 39 organizations, 28 unresolved types); 328 meet the strict human-appearance denominator. `.39` added the first 100 ASOS cards, `.40` added 101 more, and `.41` added eight people, including Coldhands; seven of those candidates have 18 clear ledger locators. The current ASOS ledger contains 82 partial chapter rows, 1,095 links and 455 unique refs. Its `.40` baseline had 263 clear missing-card relations; `.41` resolves 18 and leaves 219 after 26 P1 place relations were dispositioned, while 146 source rows remain separately excluded/ambiguous. A second pass reviewed 35 remaining P1 place candidates and justified no standalone cards. The package remains GM-only and requires a chapter frontier before any player-facing use. These are structural/index counts, not coverage percentages: all three book ledgers remain `secondary-indexed-partial`, and primary-edition verification remains open. Version `.38` added 23 named-person cards, 37 place cards and one explicitly unexecuted plan card for AGOT, plus 11 mention locators to existing cards; `.39`–`.41` add ASOS batches described above. The broad key index for books 2–5 still contains 66 cards. The setting remains GM-only: direct cards, fields, links, search, events and chronology lack a chapter frontier. The `organization_ids` column tracks houses and organizations separately from people. `people_in_dream_ids` and `places_in_dream_ids` record figures and settings represented in dreams, not the dreamers themselves; ordinary appearance/scene fields record waking scenes, and the same entity may appear in both contexts. `scene_place_ids` records scene locations; `places_referenced_ids` records named locations mentioned in reports or conversation. `events_occurred_ids` records actions and information reaching a character in the chapter, while `events_referenced_ids` records earlier events, rumors or plans. This separates the time an event occurs from the time someone learns of it. Use stable English IDs and semicolons in these fields; do not infer dates from chapter order or copy novel text into the ledger. Before changing any row to reviewed, verify it against the chosen book edition and record the edition in the research notes. Chapter title aliases were normalized to canonical numeric pages after an `Eddard V` → `Eddard VI` redirect mismatch; see [`chapter-locator-normalization-2026-11-24.md`](chapter-locator-normalization-2026-11-24.md).
