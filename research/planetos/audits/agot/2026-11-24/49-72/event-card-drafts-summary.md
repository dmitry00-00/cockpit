# AGOT chapter 56 event card drafts

Read-only draft of four short event cards for integration review. The AWOIAF numeric page for Tyrion VII lists all four names under its Events section. The cards therefore preserve only reference-only links to chapter 56; they do not claim that these events occur in the chapter or add unstated details or dates.

Primary-edition verification is pending (`secondary-indexed-partial`). Russian names are working transfers and need confirmation against the selected edition. Attribution and CC BY-SA 3.0 source metadata are included in each card. No project files were changed.

IDs: `event:burning-of-pinkmaiden-castle`, `event:burning-of-stone-hedge`, `event:fall-of-raventree-hall`, `event:yielding-of-harrenhal`. The last remains distinct from `event:tywin-leaves-harrenhal`.
