# ASOS places and organizations — draft set

## Scope and status

Read-only draft against Planetos package snapshot `.39`. It covers A Storm of Swords chapter ranges 0–20, 21–40, and 61–81. The 41–60 range remains covered by the separate seven-place and six-organization draft already supplied.

All locators come from AWOIAF chapter pages and are marked `secondary-indexed-partial`; the selected novel edition has not been checked. The summaries are original short statements based on the chapter index and its synopsis/scene metadata. No absolute dates or coordinates are added. Chapter 81 is the epilogue; its canonical URL is the Epilogue page rather than a `Chapter_81` slug.

## Draft cards: places

**Total: 25 unique P1 places.** Scene relation is stored in `locators`; `placeType` separately marks routes, vessels, towers, settlements, and other categories. No dream-, mention-only, or reference-only place is promoted to a scene locator here.

## Organizations

No new organization card met the requested P0/P1 threshold. The reconciled candidates in these ranges are P2, already represented by the live package, alias-review candidates, reference-only historical groups, or broad/temporary collectives. Notable deferrals:

- `organization:house-karstark` and `organization:queens-men` are named but P2; retain for a later pass.
- `organization:kingswood-brotherhood` and `organization:salladhor-saans-fleet` are historical/reference-only in these locators.
- `organization:bloody-mummers` needs alias review against Brave Companions.
- `organization:gold-cloaks` appears to overlap the existing City Watch of King’s Landing card.
- Unnamed retinues, garrisons, crowds, and host components are excluded as temporary or underspecified.

## Existing cards and excluded aliases

- Excluded because already present in `.39`: High Heart, Queenscrown, Oldstones, Nightfort, Black Gate, and existing major castles/cities.
- `place:wall` resolves to existing `place:the-wall`; `place:eyrie` to `place:the-eyrie`; `place:moon-door` to `place:moon-door-of-the-eyrie`.
- `place:balerion` is a vessel and remains distinct from `person:balerion-the-cat`.
- Generic or temporary interiors/sites (unnamed inns, chambers, cells, halls, tents, camps, yards, and rooms) are not promoted to durable place cards.
- `place:castle-sept` and `place:white-sword-tower` carry review notes for their exact nested/room-level location; their refs remain provisional.

## Validation

- 25 unique refs; no overlap with place/org refs in the readable `.39` content layers.
- Every locator includes book, numeric `chapterNo`, chapter title, `relation: scene`, and the canonical AWOIAF chapter URL (Prologue and Epilogue use their canonical special-page slugs).
- Every card has `status: secondary-indexed-partial`, CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution, and `verification.primaryBook: pending`.
- All links resolve to a current `.39` place card or another card in this draft set. No absolute dates or coordinates were introduced.

## Source limits

AWOIAF chapter pages are a secondary index. Their chapter-level place lists may combine scene settings, route segments, ships, references, and nested places. These drafts preserve scene relation while keeping place subtype separate; exact micro-location, alias, and typology decisions require checking against the selected novel edition.
