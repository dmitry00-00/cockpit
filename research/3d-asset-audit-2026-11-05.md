# 3D asset register audit — 2026-11-05

Scope: public listing and dataset metadata for A-001–A-016 and T-001–T-004 in the owner-provided `Реестр_3D_ассетов.xlsx`. The workbook itself and any downloaded source packages remain outside the repository. This is a metadata and rights screen, not a local import, geometry inspection, or legal opinion.

## Decision summary

- First isolated import candidates, if the owner already has the files and the relevant entitlement: **A-004 Medieval Village Pack** and **A-005 Modular Dungeon Pack**. Quaternius' current QAL allows commercial products but prohibits redistribution of the assets as assets; preserve the exact source/version and license in the asset manifest. Neither pack has been inspected locally for scale, pivots, normals, UVs, materials, LOD, collision, or target-renderer behavior.
- **A-003 Medieval Village MegaKit** is a second candidate for prototyping, with a version/content caveat: the live page advertises a source edition and engine-specific packs; the advertised 304-model total must not be assumed to be included in every free download.
- **A-001** is a strong commercial environment candidate only after confirming which license and file-format entitlement applies to the actual acquisition. Fab's Standard License permits commercial products and collaboration but disallows standalone redistribution of the asset. The public listing does not establish that this specific asset is available in a Godot-ready source format.
- Do not use **A-002** as a direct Godot-import baseline: the Asset Store listing classifies it as an Extension Asset and the compatibility table omits URP. Obtain/inspect the actual Unity package and its applicable extension terms before any conversion or collaborator sharing.
- The Fab entries need acquisition-specific license and format evidence. A marketplace's general EULA is not proof that an owner has acquired the asset, that a particular source format is included, or that source files may be delivered to every player.
- **T-001 GEDTM30** is a useful Earth terrain-method/reference dataset, not a Toril height source. It is 30 m, WGS84/EPSG:4326, vertical WGS84 ellipsoid/EGM2008 geoid, CC BY 4.0, and ML-derived; check coverage and method limits for any use.
- **T-004 Toril GIS DEM v3** is the most relevant fantasy-world terrain candidate. It is a hand-authored cartographic interpretation in custom Toril GCS, not canonical or surveyed elevation. The project FAQ states personal, non-commercial use under WotC Fan Content Policy; exclude it from a paid product/store package and do not assume a public product launch is allowed.
- **T-002** is only a catalog/access route, not a named dataset or license grant. Evaluate a specific collection and its metadata before use.
- **T-003 USGS 3DEP** is public-domain, free and unrestricted but geographically covers the United States (plus product-specific coverage such as Alaska); it is not source terrain for Toril or Planetos.

## Source notes

- Marketplace terms: [Fab Standard License](https://www.fab.com/eula?lang=en) allows project use and collaboration, disallows standalone asset redistribution; tiers share the same rights scope. The asset's selected entitlement still matters.
- [Unity Asset Store terms](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms) restrict asset redistribution except as expressly permitted by the EULA/provider license; an Extension Asset has additional conditions.
- [Quaternius Asset License v1.0](https://quaternius.com/license.html), dated 2026-08-28, grants use in commercial products but forbids standalone asset/asset-pack redistribution.
- [OpenTopography GEDTM30 metadata](https://portal.opentopography.org/datasetMetadata?otCollectionID=OT.082025.4326.1) exposes dataset-level license, CRS, vertical reference, resolution and citation.
- [USGS 3DEP product page](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services) documents product coverage/resolutions and public-domain status.
- [Toril GIS terrain model](https://www.geospatial-grimoire.com/worlds/toril/gis/docs/terrain-model/) and [FAQ](https://www.geospatial-grimoire.com/worlds/toril/gis/docs/faq/) document custom CRS, modeled DEM limits and non-commercial Fan Content terms.
