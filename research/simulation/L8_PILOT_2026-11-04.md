# L8 military-number candidate pilot — 2026-11-04

## Contract and safety boundary

`scripts/extract_military_candidates.py` streams the local MediaWiki XML and emits JSONL candidates to stdout by default. `--out` is allowed only outside the repository. It does not edit content, produce accepted facts, assign a subject/year/amount/unit/force type, or activate world state. Each candidate preserves the exact sentence-like raw wikitext, page title, revision ID/timestamp, section, source URL, license attribution, source-page wikitext SHA-256, and line/column. Numeric and spelled-out mentions are preserved as written; year-like mentions are reported separately. Approximation words and ranges stay in their original form. Reference, HTML, and wiki markup are kept; the parser never strips tags before extracting numbers.

`research/simulation/l8-pilot-review.json` is a separate manual review aid. It records `source_excerpt_verified: true` but every item has `accepted_fact: false`; it is not an import file. Unknown years are JSON `null`, while broad or approximate wording stays in `reported_time_text`. A review candidate cannot change the campaign or world.

## Manually checked source excerpts

The review JSON records revision, timestamp, SHA-256, exact excerpt and source citation markers for each item. Examples:

- **Purple Dragons, total:** “The Purple Dragons numbered over 12,000 soldiers ''circa'' 1367 DR…” (`Purple Dragons`, `Numbers`; revision `1106859`, 2026-09-15; cited as `AGTotR-p43`, *Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting 2nd edition (revised)/A Grand Tour of the Realms*, p.43). Preserve `over` and `circa`; this is not an exact count or exact year.
- **Cormyr/Suzail garrison:** “In the mid-to-late 1300s DR, the garrison numbered a large force of 4,500 soldiers…” The preceding sentence identifies the Citadel of the Purple Dragons in Suzail. The year remains `null`; this is a local garrison, not the total army. Citation markers include `FRA-p108`, `AGTotR-p44,55` and `Corm-p9,12`.
- **Arabel garrison:** “In the city of [[Arabel]] in the late 1350s and 1360s DR, the garrison numbered 2,020 men…” The source gives a span, not one year: `year: null`.
- **Eveningstar contingent:** “In the village of [[Eveningstar]] in the late 1350s DR, a contingent of nine Purple Dragons led the local militia.” The source gives only a decade: `year: null`; citation marker `Dr157-p64` resolves to *Dragon* 157, “Next Stop: Eveningstar”, p.64.
- **Luskan:** “As of 1370 DR, Luskan had an army of 300 spearmen and a navy of 19 dragonships.” The title and wording name Luskan and the year. The adjacent sentence says “The dragonships were each armed with 70 archers” and cites *Volo’s Guide to the North*, pp.112–113. The second sentence itself gives no year or subject name, so both remain `null` pending review.
- **Ambiguous Cormyr wording:** the `Cormyr` article’s Purple Dragons subsection says “In the 14th century DR, the Dragons numbered at more then 12.000 soldiers.” The source punctuation is preserved as `12.000`; it is not converted into `12,000`. This conflicts in wording with the separate Purple Dragons article and stays `needs_review`.

These are checks of the exact wiki XML excerpts and their citation markup. The referenced books themselves were not independently inspected in this pilot.

## Extractor limits and next review

The lexical pass has expected false positives: e.g. “eight days” near an army mention, page numbers in references, image dimensions, rank thresholds, ship counts, and generic numbers near a military word. All remain visibly unresolved and none is accepted automatically. It can miss numbers written in unsupported forms and cannot resolve coreference, whether a count is current, which force category a phrase means, or whether a book citation supports a particular clause. The old boolean `quantity_candidate` means only a nearby numeric and military-term mention, not a validated count.

Review candidates against the exact excerpt and cited source before creating any separate fact registry. A faction/force type, unit, date precision, and subject must have explicit evidence. No number may be inferred from “legion”, ranks, rules, or unit descriptions. No candidate triggers S4 or any other simulation.
